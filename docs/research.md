# Research & References

## Crop Disease Identification
- **PlantVillage Dataset**: Deep learning benchmarking for 38 distinct crop-disease pairs.
- **Model Architectures**: MobileNetV3 / EfficientNet for edge-friendly inference; ConvNeXt and ViT for cloud-based high-accuracy classification.

## Risk Prediction Methodology
- Correlation models between microclimate indices (leaf wetness hours, temperature fluctuations, relative humidity thresholds) and fungal/bacterial proliferation kinetics.
- Historical epidemiological models for potato late blight (e.g., Wallin model) and downy mildew.
